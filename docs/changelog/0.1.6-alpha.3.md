# 0.1.6-alpha.3

Changes since 0.1.4-alpha.3.

Version 0.1.5-alpha.3 was not published.

## Added

- Texture mipmaps and a Video filtering setting, up to 16x anisotropic filtering, to reduce distant floor shimmer. The default is 8x; menus and movies retain their original sampling.
- General healthbars, an enemy healthbar toggle, and an Auto-Use Item toggle in Combat settings.
- A native marker mouse cursor, scaled and rotated with its click hotspot at the pointed tip.
- Controller vibration when destroying generators.
- Versioned changelogs, also used as the GitHub release notes.

## Fixed

- Windows could report the game as Not Responding after returning to its window. Controller polling and graphics waits now keep window messages responsive.
- Enemies could become wedged in Cavern's narrow fire-spout passage. Collision now uses their actual body clearance without disabling nearby walls or fences.
- Grounded Generals could charge or be knocked off Sky Dominion's starting boat onto its lower hull.
- Gargoyle breath contact uses the player's full collision bounds. Reflective Shield now returns combatant projectiles, including gargoyle fireballs.
- Chests and other floor-bound objects now attach to moving platforms at their initial positions, including the affected G3/G4 chests and Maze of Illusion traps.
- Inventory controls remain available while knocked down. Anti-Death's activation sound no longer restarts on every contact while equipped.
- Breakaway walls retain their baked lighting instead of standing out from surrounding scenery.
- Completed crystal collections are announced when returning to the Tower, after the arrival sequence.
- Sumner's movement and morph animation now interpolate between gameplay updates.
- Transporter artwork has a small ground clearance to prevent z-fighting.
- Heat shimmer covers more flame particles and animated fire scenery when Bloom is enabled.
- Scroll pop-ups display "Press any button to continue" and accept fresh keyboard, controller, or mouse input. Shop Continue labels and their click targets are centered within each lane.
- Inventory item names sit lower, just above the runestone area. Corrected the Auto-Use Item label.

## Still under investigation

- The reported Courtyard tentacle contact miss has not been reproduced in the current regression tests; this release does not claim to resolve every missed swipe.
- A full-scene G4 traversal test can still stall among enemies at 60 Hz, despite the moving-platform attachment fixes.
